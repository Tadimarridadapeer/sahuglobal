01 / HERITAGE & ORIGIN

Since 1979, Sahu Global has built an enduring legacy of technical rigor, sustainable textile innovation, and uncompromising manufacturing integrity.

Founded in 1979 as an export-driven apparel house in India, we have evolved over more than four decades into a modern manufacturing partner for international fashion brands across North America and Europe.

OUR HERITAGE • SINCE 1979

Four Decades of Manufacturing Integrity

Founded in 1979, Sahu Global Private Limited has grown from an artisanal textile exporter into a modern, technologically advanced garment manufacturing enterprise serving leading fashion brands across the globe.

The Sahu Global Legacy

In 1979, Sahu Global was established with a singular conviction: that Indian textile heritage could be united with international manufacturing precision. Over four decades, that vision has evolved through successive waves of industrial automation, strict global compliance frameworks, and digital supply chain integration.

Today, Sahu Global stands as a reliable, fully compliant apparel export partner. We do not simply fulfill cut-and-sew contracts; we act as a collaborative product development and production partner for sourcing heads who demand high fidelity, predictable deliveries, and complete social accountability.

From raw fiber evaluation and CAD 3D virtual sampling to lean sewing floors and AQL final audits, our operations reflect four decades of continuous refinement.

Four Decades Timeline

1979
Foundation in India

Inauguration of export operations focusing on natural fiber apparel and hand-loomed textiles.

1992
European Expansion

Initiated long-term partnerships with UK and German fashion buying houses, establishing strict European compliance.

2005
Technology Modernization

Introduced computerized CAD pattern grading, tensionless automated spreading, and specialized finishing units.

2016
Sustainability Infrastructure

Commissioned full-scale Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) and integrated rooftop solar energy arrays.

2024–Present
Digital 3D Sampling Revolution

Implemented Clo3D virtual draping and end-to-end digitized production telemetry for international brands.

QUALITY VERIFICATION

Quality Assurance & AQL Standards

At Sahu Global, quality is engineered into every stage from incoming yarn to export palletization. We operate strictly under ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 (ISO 2859-1) AQL 1.5/2.5 benchmarks.

4-Point Fabric Inspection

Every fabric roll is inspected on illuminated D65 inspection frames before releasing to the cutting floor.

In-Line Statistical Audits

Roving QA inspectors conduct hourly random sampling across sewing lines to preempt tension or stitch defects.

Conveyor Needle Detection

100% of finished garments pass through dual-head conveyorized metal detectors for child and consumer safety.

Pre-Shipment AQL 1.5 Gate

Independent final QA clearance verifying measurements, packaging, barcodes, and carton drop integrity.
